Community Coalitions

Built by the community. Guided by the community.

Community Coalitions is a new project helping local people, organisations, venues, groups and services connect more easily.

Our first coalition is taking shape in North West Bristol, beginning with Henbury & Brentry, Southmead and Lawrence Weston.

We are building a community-owned local network, a searchable community atlas, shared events listings, creative projects, youth opportunities, health and fitness activity, and practical support for stronger local roots.
